Emergency alerts

3 alerts
BLSCode BlueCardiac or respiratory emergency · respond within your trainingMedical

At a glance: A person may be unresponsive, not breathing normally, or in cardiac arrest. Activate the response immediately under local policy.

  • Call the facility's emergency number and state the exact location; ask someone to bring the AED or emergency equipment.
  • Begin CPR or other immediate care only within your current training and assigned role; follow dispatcher or response-team direction.
  • Keep the area clear, direct responders in, and share known information when the team arrives.
FIRECode RedFire or smoke · protect people and follow the site fire planFire / smoke

At a glance: Report the exact location and whether you see fire or smoke. Follow your facility's fire response and evacuation plan.

  • Activate the alarm and call the facility's emergency number as directed by local policy.
  • Move people from immediate danger; close doors if safe and keep routes clear.
  • Use an extinguisher only if trained, the fire is small, and you have a clear exit. Evacuate if unsafe.
TRAUMATrauma AlertSerious injury · activate using approved criteria and pathwayInjury

At a glance: Use the hospital's trauma activation criteria and notify the response team with the available information.

  • Share location, mechanism of injury, estimated arrival or event time, and known patient details when available.
  • Ensure access to the patient and keep the route clear for responders.
  • Provide immediate care only within your training and role; avoid moving the person unless there is immediate danger.
